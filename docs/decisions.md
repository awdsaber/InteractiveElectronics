# Decisions

- 2026-11-06 — 3D ink outlines use drei `<Outlines>` in pixel mode (`screenspace={false}`, ~1.6 CSS px × dpr) on a cached crease-smoothed hull geometry, not the spec's 0.02 world-unit thickness, so line weight stays pen-like at every zoom and box corners close.
- 2026-11-06 — Ink wobble samples noise(worldPos·4 + time·0.5) per spec but applies it as a screen-plane offset of ≤1.25 px (time stepped at 8 fps); the spec's 0.01-unit displacement is sub-pixel, and a depth-free offset keeps decals and stacked faces from z-fighting. Decal materials get a wobble-scaled polygon offset.
- 2026-11-06 — Board/PCB/mat artwork (mask, traces, pads, silkscreen, grid) is one canvas texture on the extruded slab's top cap, not a separate decal plane.
- 2026-11-06 — Part coordinates: origin at the board centre, PCB top at y = 0, 1 unit = 10 mm; `pos` [x, z] or [x, y, z]; off-board parts default to the bench top (y = −0.18).
- 2026-11-06 — Connectors (USB-B/C, micro-USB, barrel jack, screw terminal) open toward +Z (the front edge the iso camera sees) at rot 0; the Uno-style module turns so its connector edge faces +Z. Electrolytic stripe/negative lead and diode cathode bands sit on +X for the same reason.
- 2026-11-06 — `PartRenderState` adds optional `rpm`, `value` and `reading` for live motor speed, servo angle / pot position / button state, and display text.
- 2026-11-07 — Part bodies stay authored as many small InkMeshes; `<Bake>` (in every part frame) merges a part's opaque untextured meshes into one vertex-coloured draw + one merged hull at mount, rebaking when the part's params/state key changes. Moving groups opt out with `userData.noBake`. 40-part lesson ≈ 170 draws.
- 2026-11-07 — Plates thinner than 0.05 units use 1 px EdgesGeometry lines instead of an outline hull; materials on per-instance (owner-disposed) textures bypass the shared material cache.
- 2026-11-07 — Lessons open on the finished, unpowered board (READY); "Strip to bare PCB" starts the build. On `base: 'breadboard-only'` benches there is no PCB, no SMD and no solder step; power still needs every part placed.
- 2026-11-07 — Extra power sources are `Lesson.extraPower: {elementId, label}[]` (one labelled button each) rather than `powerElementIds`; the main `powerElementId` also powers the MCU. Power off sets `disabled: true` on the source.
- 2026-11-07 — Status lines: burns, then active failures (their `explain`), then thermal shutdown, then the lesson's `statusLines` with the last match winning, then a default per status. `pin` conditions match only while the sketch drives the pin.
- 2026-11-07 — MCU pin changes reach the circuit at most one frame late: each frame steps the sim, then starts one MCU advance to the new sim time (skipped while the previous one is pending). checkRatings runs once per frame. Between a netlist change and the next step, readouts come from a DC operating point.
- 2026-11-07 — A burnt GPIO (`pin-overcurrent`) does not char its `module` part; the status line reports the dead pin. Bench callouts stay mounted and toggle `hidden` (drei Html roots are costly and racy to unmount); `params.callout` offsets untangle crowded labels.
- 2026-11-07 — Lessons and behaviors register by file glob (`src/lessons/T*/*.ts` exporting `lesson`, `src/engine/behaviors/*.ts` exporting `behavior`); sidebar order comes from `TRACK_ORDER` in `src/lessons/index.ts`. Authoring guides: `src/lessons/README.md`, `src/engine/behaviors/README.md`.
- 2026-11-07 — Failures can swap 3D parts: `FailureScenario.parts {hide, show}` plus `PartPlacement.hidden` for parts that exist only while a failure shows them (not counted in PARTS). Status lines may interpolate readouts with `{n}`.
- 2026-11-07 — Breadboard benches show "Clear the breadboard" and no Pick & place / Solder buttons.
- 2026-11-07 — led-resistor deviates from its curriculum board table: `base: 'breadboard-only'` (R1 and the LED sit in a breadboard, so nothing is soldered), size 16 × 8.5 for camera framing, and no separate `usb` part (the Uno module draws its own USB-B; `V_USB` maps to `uno`).
- 2026-11-07 — esp32-s3 deviates from its curriculum: `base: 'breadboard-only'` with the DevKitC-1 built as a generic `module` carrier plus SMD/THT sub-parts (the engine draws the PCB at board.size, so a small soldered DevKit and a breadboard alongside can't share a `mat` bench); no `led_pwr` part (the carrier draws its own red PWR LED) and no exploded-view-only crystal; the pot is one `POT` element tweaked by `pos` (not two legs with a 10–9990 Ω lower leg); GPIO0 gets an explicit 45 kΩ internal pull-up `r_pu0`; `pin4` has no 3.6 V `maxV` (overvoltage burns instantly, which would make the curriculum's own 'GPIO4 above VDD + 0.3 V' warning unreachable), so in F1 GPIO4 dies with `d_esd` after 300 ms of > 5 mA; the 5 V test signal toggles once a second (a 2 s period) so the 1 s report sees both levels; `serial-plotter` is not listed (not registered yet).
- 2026-11-07 — Behaviors can print to the serial monitor (`ctx.mcu.serialOut`), and a held MCU's own Serial output is dropped. Parts with `params.rgbFrom: '<instance>'` glow in the colour that behavior publishes as `rgb_r/g/b` (`src/three/rgbGlow.ts`); `ledLook` accepts `#rrggbb`.
