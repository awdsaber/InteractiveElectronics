# Decisions

- 2026-11-06 — 3D ink outlines use drei `<Outlines>` in pixel mode (`screenspace={false}`, ~1.6 CSS px × dpr) on a cached crease-smoothed hull geometry, not the spec's 0.02 world-unit thickness, so line weight stays pen-like at every zoom and box corners close.
- 2026-11-06 — Ink wobble samples noise(worldPos·4 + time·0.5) per spec but applies it as a screen-plane offset of ≤1.25 px (time stepped at 8 fps); the spec's 0.01-unit displacement is sub-pixel, and a depth-free offset keeps decals and stacked faces from z-fighting. Decal materials get a wobble-scaled polygon offset.
- 2026-11-06 — Board/PCB/mat artwork (mask, traces, pads, silkscreen, grid) is one canvas texture on the extruded slab's top cap, not a separate decal plane.
- 2026-11-06 — Part coordinates: origin at the board centre, PCB top at y = 0, 1 unit = 10 mm; `pos` [x, z] or [x, y, z]; off-board parts default to the bench top (y = −0.18).
- 2026-11-06 — Connectors (USB-B/C, micro-USB, barrel jack, screw terminal) open toward +Z (the front edge the iso camera sees) at rot 0; the Uno-style module turns so its connector edge faces +Z. Electrolytic stripe/negative lead and diode cathode bands sit on +X for the same reason.
- 2026-11-06 — `PartRenderState` adds optional `rpm`, `value` and `reading` for live motor speed, servo angle / pot position / button state, and display text.
- 2026-11-07 — Part bodies stay authored as many small InkMeshes; `<Bake>` (in every part frame) merges a part's opaque untextured meshes into one vertex-coloured draw + one merged hull at mount, rebaking when the part's params/state key changes. Moving groups opt out with `userData.noBake`. 40-part lesson ≈ 170 draws.
- 2026-11-07 — Plates thinner than 0.05 units use 1 px EdgesGeometry lines instead of an outline hull; materials on per-instance (owner-disposed) textures bypass the shared material cache.
- 2026-11-07 — Lessons open on the finished, unpowered board (READY); "Strip to bare PCB" starts the build. On `base: 'breadboard-only'` benches there is no PCB, no SMD and no solder step; power still needs every part placed.
- 2026-11-07 — Extra power sources are `Lesson.extraPower: {elementId, label}[]` (one labelled button each) rather than `powerElementIds`; the main `powerElementId` also powers the MCU. Power off sets `disabled: true` on the source.
- 2026-11-07 — Status lines: burns, then active failures (their `explain`), then thermal shutdown, then the lesson's `statusLines` with the last match winning, then a default per status. `pin` conditions match only while the sketch drives the pin.
- 2026-11-07 — MCU pin changes reach the circuit at most one frame late: each frame steps the sim, then starts one MCU advance to the new sim time (skipped while the previous one is pending). checkRatings runs once per frame. Between a netlist change and the next step, readouts come from a DC operating point.
- 2026-11-07 — A burnt GPIO (`pin-overcurrent`) does not char its `module` part; the status line reports the dead pin. Bench callouts stay mounted and toggle `hidden` (drei Html roots are costly and racy to unmount); `params.callout` offsets untangle crowded labels.
- 2026-11-07 — Lessons and behaviors register by file glob (`src/lessons/T*/*.ts` exporting `lesson`, `src/engine/behaviors/*.ts` exporting `behavior`); sidebar order comes from `TRACK_ORDER` in `src/lessons/index.ts`. Authoring guides: `src/lessons/README.md`, `src/engine/behaviors/README.md`.
- 2026-11-07 — Failures can swap 3D parts: `FailureScenario.parts {hide, show}` plus `PartPlacement.hidden` for parts that exist only while a failure shows them (not counted in PARTS). Status lines may interpolate readouts with `{n}`.
- 2026-11-07 — Breadboard benches show "Clear the breadboard" and no Pick & place / Solder buttons.
- 2026-11-07 — led-resistor deviates from its curriculum board table: `base: 'breadboard-only'` (R1 and the LED sit in a breadboard, so nothing is soldered), size 16 × 8.5 for camera framing, and no separate `usb` part (the Uno module draws its own USB-B; `V_USB` maps to `uno`).
- 2026-11-07 — `Behavior.onSerial(ctx, line)` receives every complete Serial line (not the `[mcu]` warnings); `serial-plotter` builds on it. `BoardSpec.frame {size, center}` sets what the camera frames and the mat covers when a breadboard sits beside a PCB.
- 2026-11-07 — In a transient circuit, MCU reads (`digitalRead`, `analogRead`) after ANY pin change since the last step come from `Transient.peek(h)`: one uncommitted backward-Euler step from the current C/L state over the MCU time since the change. A pull-up reads HIGH at once on a bare pin but not through an RC, and cross-wired pins see each other. `Readout.floor` clamps tiny values to 0; status text names remaining parts by label or count (`describeParts`), never raw ids.
- 2026-11-07 — arduino-uno deviates from its curriculum: TQFP-32 ATmega328P (SMD edition) instead of the socketed DIP-28, radial THT electrolytics; `i_board` is a 110 Ω load (an ideal I source drags the unplugged rail negative); `f1` is a FUSE (0.5 A hold, i2t 10 A²s); the pot is a POT element turned by `pot-knob`; pin 9 has no `vccNode` (averaged PWM would draw phantom shoot-through from the rail), so pin-driven LED current is not in USB CURRENT.
- 2026-11-07 — Opt-in `lesson.sim.edgeSubsteps` (`TransientOptions.edgeSubsteps`): the first step after a pin, parameter or netlist change is split into n backward-Euler substeps growing ×2, and the bench checks ratings on each substep. It resolves µs events such as an inductive kick into a transistor's `vBreak` clamp at the default 1 ms step (transistor-switch F1); lessons without it are unchanged. Substep samples use `checkRatings(..., { instantOnly: true })` (maxV only; no heat, means, timers or lastT), so per-frame thermal and burn-delay timing is unaffected. The lead approved it as opt-in per lesson; the bench never enables it automatically.
- 2026-11-07 — transistor-switch deviates from its curriculum: `base: 'breadboard-only'`, no separate `usb` part (`V_USB` maps to `uno`), Q1 gets `vBreak: 60` (between V_CEO 40 V and V_CBO 75 V), board size 18 × 14 (not 18 × 11) to frame the pack and motor behind the breadboard, D1 lies in the breadboard (front row e, column 15 to column 19, jumpered to +6 V) rather than at the motor terminals, the V_CE > 1 V warning becomes Q1 POWER > 0.05 W (V_CE sits near 6 V whenever Q1 is simply off; saturated Q1 dissipates ≈ 33 mW, R_B = 47 kΩ ≈ 80 mW), the load slider stops at 0.002 N·m (≈ 0.5 A, under Q1's 600 mA), and a fifth failure drives the motor straight from pin 9.
- 2026-11-07 — `tb6612` behavior: each TB6612FNG output is an averaged half-bridge (V source = duty × VM referenced to the driver ground, behind Ron/2, then a SW that opens for high-Z), set every tick from pin voltages read against VIH/VIL relative to `params.gnd`. BRIDGE LOSS and Tj add the PWM ripple analytically (var = min(D(1−D)(VM/R)², ΔI²/12), ΔI = VM·D(1−D)/(L·f)), so 980 Hz drive/brake PWM heats by RMS rather than average current. Channel B and several instances are supported for the capstone. "No common ground" is a heuristic (netlist DC path plus |V(gnd)| ≤ 1 V), not a solver feature.
- 2026-11-07 — tb6612fng deviates from its curriculum: `tauTh` 2 s, time-compressed from "≈ 5 s" (a real breakout is tens of seconds), plus the ripple-aware loss, so F1's thermal shutdown trips within one sketch cycle; the sketch ramps to ±255 (not ±200), so the ≈ 190 rpm full-speed figure shows and F1 trips; the battery is an `extraPower` button, not a `bat_sw` toggle tweak; the bridge sources, `i_vm` and `c_vm` reference `bat_n` (the driver ground) rather than '0'; `i_board`/`i_vcc` become resistors `r_board` (111 Ω) and `r_vcc` (4.5 kΩ to `bat_n`) so a battery-only bench solves; BRIDGE LOSS is the behavior's `bridge_W` (both halves) instead of `ron_a` power; `fig: 5` (the video figure); board 21 × 11.5 (not 14 × 8) to fit the parts at scale; `drv` is a silkscreen label rather than a module; `mcu-supply` and `serial-plotter` are not listed yet.
- 2026-11-07 — esp32-s3 deviates from its curriculum: `base: 'breadboard-only'` with the DevKitC-1 built as a generic `module` carrier plus SMD/THT sub-parts (the engine draws the PCB at board.size, so a small soldered DevKit and a breadboard alongside can't share a `mat` bench); no `led_pwr` part (the carrier draws its own red PWR LED) and no exploded-view-only crystal; the pot is one `POT` element tweaked by `pos` (not two legs with a 10–9990 Ω lower leg); GPIO0 gets an explicit 45 kΩ internal pull-up `r_pu0`; `pin4` has no 3.6 V `maxV` (overvoltage burns instantly, which would make the curriculum's own 'GPIO4 above VDD + 0.3 V' warning unreachable), so in F1 GPIO4 dies with `d_esd` after 300 ms of > 5 mA; the 5 V test signal is a 0.5 Hz square wave (1 s high, 1 s low) from the reusable `square-wave` behavior, so the 1 s report sees both levels; the pot starts at 85 % so the PWM LED is visibly lit despite the bench's voltage-averaged PWM; `serial-plotter` is not listed (not registered yet).
- 2026-11-07 — Behaviors can print to the serial monitor (`ctx.mcu.serialOut`), and a held MCU's own Serial output is dropped. Parts with `params.rgbFrom: '<instance>'` glow in the colour that behavior publishes as `rgb_r/g/b` (`src/three/rgbGlow.ts`), carried in `PartRenderState.glowColor` (outside the bake key, so colour changes never rebake); `ledLook` accepts `#rrggbb`. A `button` with `params.pressedWhen: '<failure id>'` draws pressed while that failure is active.
- 2026-11-07 — Behaviors get every sketch pin change with its MCU timestamp through an optional `Behavior.onPin(ctx, pin, state, tMicros)` (called synchronously inside the MCU's advance, only while the MCU is live), so STEP/DIR drivers see µs pulse trains that the per-frame `ctx.pins` would alias; `ctx.burn(id, reason, { value, limit })` lets a behavior state what killed a part for the FAULT text. New readout unit `steps` (no SI prefix). A `stepper-nema17` with `params.angleKey`/`rpmKey` takes its shaft angle (°, continuous) and speed from published values, and runs on at that speed between the ~15 Hz snapshots (≤ 0.1 s ahead).
- 2026-11-07 — `a4988` behavior: the chopper is averaged (each coil an I source following I_trip·cos/sin of the 64-position table, rising no faster than (±VMOT − I·(Rcoil + Rds) − back-EMF)/L, fast decay when reversing); the rotor is a 50-tooth hybrid model, T = Km·(I_B cos φ − I_A sin φ) with Km = holdTorque/iRated and back-EMF constant = Km, Coulomb load and friction, viscous damping 1e-3 N·m·s/rad; edges and rotor integrate in ≤ 20 µs slices on the MCU's clock. Skipped steps = 4 full steps per electrical cycle the rotor falls behind the translator. Damage above the 2 A absolute maximum after 0.75 s in total, and the open-coil kick (L·I/10 µs against 35 V), are teaching rules, not datasheet figures. Pull-out with this model: ≈ 640 rpm at 12 V, ≈ 1070 rpm at 24 V (1/8 step, 0.05 N·m load).
- 2026-11-07 — a4988-stepper deviates from its curriculum: the Vref trimmer is one `POT` element tweaked by `pos` (0.02–0.99) instead of `pot_top`/`pot_bot` legs, because a single-leg tweak can't keep the sum at 10 kΩ (pot_bot 9900 Ω alone gives 2.6 V, not 4.95 V); F1 sets `pos` 0.99. `pwr` is `closed: true` (the bench opens the power SW while off). `drv` is a silkscreen label with the IC, sense resistors, trimmer and header on the bench PCB (as in tb6612fng), not a module; there is no heatsink part (the only `heatsink` is a TO-220 fin, twice a stick-on's size); the board is 16 × 10.5 (not 15 × 9) to frame the 62 mm-tall motor. The windings get a thermal rating (18 °C/W, τ 30 s compressed from minutes, 130 °C class B) instead of `maxP: 10`, which would burn the coil open 0.3 s into F1, before thermal shutdown; a COIL A TEMP readout is added. The driver's τ of 8 s is time-compressed (a carrier settles over tens of seconds). In F1 VMOT sags to ≈ 10.6 V under the ≈ 11 A draw, so the coils reach ≈ 4.8 A rather than 5.5 A. In F3 the motor can't pull in at 1067 full steps/s from rest in this model, so it mostly buzzes and skips rather than turning 16 times. `fig: 7` (the video figure). `mcu-supply` and `serial-plotter` are not listed (they aren't registered on main).
- 2026-11-07 — Callout tags are drawn by one DOM overlay per canvas (`<CalloutHost>` around the `<Canvas>`, store in `src/three/calloutLayer.ts`), not a drei `<Html>` per tag: each `<Html>` owned a React root and recreated it when r3f connected its event source after the first commit, unmounting synchronously mid-render, and that deferred unmount wiped the first labelled part's tag. Every `<Callout>` must sit under a `<CalloutHost>`.
- 2026-11-07 — a4988 review round 1: `Behavior.onPin` is dispatched from the bench's `applyPin` on every change of a pin's state (sketch writes, floats on hold / stop / upload as INPUT 0, re-drive on release), MCU-timed; behaviors must not call `ctx.v` in it. `ctx.burn` detail can carry a whole `note` for the FAULT text. The `a4988` behavior now models the genuine part's overcurrent latch (I_OCPST 2.1 A min: outputs off until ENABLE is toggled or the power cycles); the over-2 A damage rule (0.5 s total) only runs with `ocp: false`, a clone without working protection. Driver RθJA is 90 °C/W (bare carrier), so thermal shutdown trips near 1.5 A per coil (≈ 88 °C at 1 A), with an RθJA tweak (40–90) for a heatsink. After an MCU clock restart (`mcu.reset()`, upload) queued entries stamped after the new time are the old run's: their levels are taken without pulse timing and the new run's edges are kept. VMOT power-off keeps the input levels (VDD is the Uno's). The open-coil burn hits the opened coil's source and quotes its ½·L·I². The lesson's 12 V adapter current-limits at 2 A (`iLimit`), F1 now shows the OCP latch (≈ 0.44 ms after the trimmer hits the stop, from 0.71 A at 12 V / 2.8 mH), COIL A TEMP comes from the behavior floored at ambient, an OVERCURRENT LATCH readout and status line are added, and the motor moves left ([-5.8, -1.7], behind the carrier in depth) with the carrier at [-0.3, 1.9], and the board grows to 17 × 12 so the shaft stays in frame.
- 2026-11-07 — `l298n` behavior: the TB6612 structure with bipolar outputs. The conducting path is fitted to the datasheet's *typical* VCE(sat) (1.35 + 1.2 = 2.55 V at 1 A, 2.0 + 1.7 = 3.7 V at 2 A; 1.8 V at 1 A is the minimum, not typical) as an offset plus a slope: `vce0` 1.4 V + `rTotal` 1.15 Ω per path. The slope is in the netlist (0.525 Ω + the closed SW's 0.05 Ω per half); the behavior subtracts the offset from the driving source, so the no-load drop is ≈ 1.57 V; brake is resistive only. EN low opens all four outputs (coast), IN1 = IN2 brakes. EN-PWM coasts in its off phase, so the averaged source is d_eff·V_on + (1 − d_eff)·back-EMF (clamped to the rails), where d_eff = d·k corrects for the winding's rise time, k = 1 − (τ/t_on)(1 − e^(−t_on/τ)), τ = L/(R_motor + rTotal), t_on = d/980 Hz (20 % duty → 52 % of full speed, matching a switched simulation's ≈ 53 %); the loss is vce0·I + R·I²/d_eff. Quiescent Vs current follows the datasheet by state (50 mA enabled with an input high, 13 mA inputs low, ≤ 4 mA EN low). With the flyback diodes missing, the outputs open for one tick per drive command (latched on mode and duty, so independent of the frame rate) and the engine resolves the kick (SW `vBreak` 60 V, `sim.edgeSubsteps` 12, `maxV` 50 V); the behavior never calls `burn`. A burnt output SW makes the channel dead: any drive command draws a (Vs − 1.4 V) / 1.15 Ω shoot-through from Vs. The 150 °C overtemperature threshold and 20 °C hysteresis are approximate (the datasheet gives none). "Heatsink fitted" is the presence of the 0.01 Ω element `hs_tab` (the grounded Multiwatt tab bonded to the heatsink), because failure patches cannot set behavior params.
- 2026-11-07 — l298n deviates from its curriculum: `tauTh` 4 s (time-compressed; a module on its heatsink takes minutes); F2 cannot patch `behavior:l298n` params, so it removes `hs_tab` (no heatsink) and shorts IN1→IN3, IN2→IN4 (channel B is always in the netlist with IN3/IN4 pulled low and ENB jumpered; it then mirrors A, and a hidden second GM-48 `mot2` appears), and it loads `m2` too; F3 also sets a 0.3 A load (`m1.load` 0.0015 N·m; with the typical 2.5 V-class drop a 0.5 A load stalls the motor on 3.7 V) so its effect shows without the slider; F1 removes all eight diodes (both channels); the battery is an `extraPower` button (closed by default), not a `bat_sw` tweak; the bridge sources and `i_vs` reference `bat_n`; the Uno's `i_board` is `r_board` as in tb6612fng; the pack is drawn as `battery-lipo` (2S, 7.4 V) rather than an AA pack; the L298 is a `regulator-to220` (3 legs, marking L298N) on a `heatsink`, and the 78M05 an 8-pin `ic-smd`; two readouts are added (OUTPUT SPEED, EFFICIENCY in %); BRIDGE DROP is the on-state drop vce0 + R·I_on (= Vs − |V_motor| at full duty); board 21 × 11.5 (not 14 × 9), like tb6612fng, with the module moved forward and the F2 motor behind the Uno so neither wheel hides the L298 and its heatsink. The curriculum's drop figures (0.95 + 0.85 = 1.8 V "typical" at 1 A) were the datasheet minimum; the curriculum is corrected to the typical 2.55 V / 3.7 V and the numbers that follow from it (≈ 4.85 V and 66 % at 1 A from 7.4 V, ≈ 5× the TB6612's loss, 204 °C bare for two channels at 1 A, F2 ≈ 9.4 W).
- 2026-11-07 — Renderer debt: the L298's Multiwatt15 package (15 staggered legs, tab bolted to the heatsink) has no part model; l298n draws it as a 3-leg `regulator-to220` marked L298N in front of a `heatsink`, and the 78M05's DPAK as an 8-pin `ic-smd`. A `multiwatt15` and a `dpak` footprint need a new PartKind (types.ts, kinds.ts, parts/index.ts).
- 2026-11-07 — `pca9685` behavior: the PCA9685 is a register file (MODE1/MODE2, LEDn_ON/OFF with the full-on/full-off bits, ALL_LED, PRE_SCALE writable only while MODE1.SLEEP, auto-increment only with MODE1.AI, All-Call 0x70 while MODE1.ALLCALL) attached to the virtual `Wire` bus at every jumper-selectable address 0x40..0x7F; only the selected one acknowledges, and nothing does while SDA/SCL idle below 0.7 VDD or VDD < 2.3 V. The `Adafruit_PWMServoDriver` library class is a real declarable type talking to the same registers (Adafruit's prescale formula and writeMicroseconds conversion with the sketch's oscillator value). Servos are a slew-limited model (600 °/s × V+/4.8 V, drive band 4°, motor lag 15 ms, current = 10 mA + 0.65 A × min(1, |drive − speed| + load) + 0.24 A × speed), loaded onto V+ as resistors V+/I; the sketch's commands between two ticks are ramped across the tick so the current does not depend on the frame rate. Servo horns render from `params.angleKey` (a published key) in `partState`.
- 2026-11-07 — Engine: `I2cDevice.ack?()` (optional, default true): a device that returns false NACKs its address (`endTransmission` returns 2, `requestFrom` returns 0). Additive; existing devices are unchanged.
- 2026-11-07 — pca9685-servo deviates from its curriculum: the sketch declares `Adafruit_PWMServoDriver pwm = Adafruit_PWMServoDriver(0x40);` instead of using a pre-made `pwm` global (library classes are now declarable); servo loads s0..s3 are resistors set to V+/I rather than I sources, and `i_board`/`i_pca` are resistors `r_board` (111 Ω) and `r_pca` (833 Ω), so a collapsed rail can't be driven negative; the servos hold a default horn load of 0.3 × stall torque (a `Horn load` tweak, 0..1), so four servos draw about 1 A and F1 really trips the Uno's polyfuse; `f1` is a thermal (shutdown-and-recover) polyfuse model (rth 600 °C/W, τ 1.5 s, 125 °C: holds 0.5 A, trips in about a second at 1 A, time-compressed) instead of `maxI 1.0`, so F1 is a boot loop rather than a permanent burn; the Uno brown-out (2.7 V) comes from `mcu-supply` (instance `mcu`) with a BROWN-OUTS readout; the servo PSU is an `extraPower` button rather than a `psu_sw` toggle tweak; no bulk-capacitor tweak (the averaged servo current makes it invisible); the servo responds above 4.0 V and twitches below 3.5 V with hysteresis between, dead below 2 V; a fourth failure `addr-jumper` (A0 bridged → 0x41, NACKs) shows the address check; CH0 PULSE reads `ch0_s` in seconds (formats as ms) and an I²C NACKS readout was added; `fig: 8` (the video figure); board 17 × 10; the 16 × 3 servo header is two header parts (`header-male` has at most 2 rows); `serial-plotter` is not listed (the sketch prints only one line, nothing to plot).
- 2026-11-07 — Callout tags are drawn by one DOM overlay per canvas (`<CalloutHost>` around the `<Canvas>`, store in `src/three/calloutLayer.ts`), not a drei `<Html>` per tag: each `<Html>` owned a React root and recreated it when r3f connected its event source after the first commit, unmounting synchronously mid-render, and that deferred unmount wiped the first labelled part's tag. - 2026-11-07 — Small additive engine hooks for `imu-mpu6050`: `BehaviorContext.read?(key)` reads another behavior's published value from a snapshot taken at the start of each behavior pass, so it lags exactly one tick in any behavior order (the capstone feeds the robot's pitch to the IMU this way); `I2cDevice.ack?()` (main's identical version, from pca9685) lets the chip move between 0x68/0x69 or drop off a dead bus; failure `set` patches on `behavior:<instance>` override that instance's params while active (validated against the instance list).
- 2026-11-07 — `imu-mpu6050`: the chip is sampled at 1 kHz per tick; each DLPF setting is a first-order low-pass with τ = the register map's delay (so mode 6 adds ≈ 19 ms), while white noise is band-limited to the setting's bandwidth (σ = density·√BW, AR(1)) and the ERM vibration (amplitude vibGain·ω², at the motor's shaft frequency, with phase jitter) is attenuated by 1/√(1 + (f/BW)²). Registers latch the last sample of each frame, so a sketch polling at 100 Hz (faster than the 60 fps frame) reads some samples twice. Noise is seeded (mulberry32 + Box–Muller). A reference complementary filter (α 0.98, 100 Hz, 1 s gyro calibration after power-on, upload and bus recovery) gives the readouts; it publishes 0 while the chip is not found at the library's address.
- 2026-11-07 — The serial monitor header shows the baud of the sketch's literal `Serial.begin(n)` (comments ignored), 9600 when there is none.
- 2026-11-07 — mpu6050-imu deviates from its curriculum: F2's text says the bench models a master without internal pull-ups (a real Uno's Wire.begin() enables 20–50 kΩ pull-ups to 5 V, above the MPU's VDD + 0.5 V limit and too weak for clean edges); `fig: 9` (the video figure); readouts come from the behavior's reference filter (`pitch_accel`, `pitch_gyro`, `pitch_filtered`) instead of `serial.*` (no `serial-plotter` behavior yet), plus a fifth pitch readout GYRO PITCH (CAL) (`pitch_gyro_cal`, power-up calibrated like the sketch) beside the curriculum's uncorrected GYRO PITCH (RAW), so both the 126°/min raw drift and F1's 54°/min calibrated drift are visible; `usb_plug` starts closed (power toggles `disabled`, as in other lessons); `i_board` is a 111 Ω resistor (an I source on 5 V blew up i_board/Q1 at power-up); a power LED (`r_led` 1 kΩ + `led_pwr`) is in the netlist; the ERM's `b` is 4e-8 (not 5e-11) so it runs at ≈ 15 000 rpm / 65 mA and stops in ≈ 50 ms instead of free-spinning for ~40 s, with vibGain 2.9e-7 g/(rad/s)² for ≈ 0.5 g rms; the GY-521 is a silkscreen area on a 13 × 10 bench PCB (no separate module part, no tilt plate: tilt comes from the Plate pitch tweak); a failure that cuts the chip off the bus resets the MCU (a re-plug) and repair resets it again, so setup()'s testConnection() check prints "not found" and recovers; the ≈ 245 Hz shake (after the DLPF) aliases to ≈ 45–50 Hz in the 100 Hz loop, where the complementary filter attenuates it ≈ 31× (an earlier ~4× figure came from the free-spinning 5e-11 motor).
